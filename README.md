# Praenox Shift

Praenox Shift is a daytime sound composition and listening app by Arlej Tech. It uses the Praenox audio engine to create adaptive soundscapes for focus, gaming, learning, calm, creativity, energy, recharge, and unwind.

## How it works

1. Choose a sound world and adjust its palette and character.
2. Generate a soundscape on your device.
3. Listen in the player, including while the phone is locked.
4. Find all generated compositions in the library, export them as WAV files, and make a portable Shift backup.
5. Optionally import an Aurum profile to personalize Shift's starting sound preferences.

## Main features

- Eight daytime sound worlds and a customizable sound palette
- Adaptive soundscape generation and animated player visualization
- Background playback with a playback notification
- Persistent library for all generated soundscapes and WAV export
- Portable ZIP backup and restore for the soundscape library and sound settings
- Optional Aurum profile import; Shift works without one
- Local settings for playback level and session duration
- Free plan with 10 listening sessions per rolling seven days; Shift Unlimited removes the limit

## Privacy

Shift generates and plays sound on the device. An imported Aurum profile is optional; Shift uses its summarized learning preferences and typical profile, not the raw health history. All generated compositions are saved in Shift's private app storage. Use **Settings → Export Shift backup** to create a portable ZIP containing the library and sound settings, and **Import Shift backup** to restore it. Android also refreshes a local recovery copy when the app leaves the foreground.
